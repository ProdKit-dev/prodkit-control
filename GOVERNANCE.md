# Governance

ProdKit Control begins with a maintainer-led governance model and aims to evolve
toward a multi-maintainer, consensus-oriented project.

## Roles

- **Contributor:** submits issues, documentation, tests, or code.
- **Reviewer:** has demonstrated domain expertise and may review pull requests.
- **Maintainer:** can merge changes, publish releases, and participate in security response.

## Decision process

Routine changes use lazy consensus. Contract-breaking changes, security-boundary changes, and
new mandatory dependencies require an architecture decision record and explicit maintainer
approval.

## Independence

The canonical core must remain usable without a required commercial provider. Commercial and
open-source integrations are welcome as replaceable adapters.
